The problem

Your answers are already in your data

Every month your business records sales, receipts, orders and production. But it sits in Excel files, system exports and paper registers that nobody has time to study. So the important questions go unanswered:

  • Which products, customers and salespeople really make your money?
  • Which good customers have gone quiet?
  • Are you producing what actually sells, or filling the store with stock that does not move?
  • Do your receipts match your sales?
  • Who should your sales team call next?

AKILI Growth Engine answers these for you, every month.

How it thinks

Two engines, one goal: growth

Inside your business

We read your own data (sales, receipts, orders, production and stock) and show you what is working, who owns the results and where money is slipping away.

Outside your business

We find new companies to sell to, research each one, and give your salesperson the right words to approach them, in Kiswahili and English.

One list for the owner
Both come together in one short monthly list of actions for you, the owner. You decide what to do.

Production

Built for manufacturers

If you produce goods, AKILI Growth Engine also reads your production records, from your ERP or simply your Excel production sheets, and shows you:

  • Output against plan for every product, line and shift
  • Who owns the results: which supervisor's shifts produce the most, with the fewest rejects
  • Material leakage: raw material used per unit, compared across shifts and months, valued in shillings
  • Rejects and downtime by machine and cause
  • Production against sales: where you are overproducing and tying up cash, and where you are running out and losing sales

Then it connects the two: products piling up in the store become offers for your sales team.

Security

Your data stays yours

Your own server

Each client's engine runs on a separate server, managed by FlowrA or on your own server if you prefer. Your data is never mixed with another company's.

Confidentiality first

We sign a confidentiality agreement and a data processing agreement before you share a single file.

AI sees summaries, not your customers

Our AI agents work with totals and summaries. Your customers' names and contact details are not sent to them.

Strong access controls

One-time login codes, automatic logout, approval by a second person for sensitive actions, and a full record of who did what.

Every number checked

Figures are calculated from your data and must match your own totals. The AI explains and recommends; it never makes up numbers.
